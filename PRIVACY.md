# Rotmarch: Hold the Road — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 1 November 2026*

Rotmarch: Hold the Road is a single-player tower defence game by Ashen Lantern Studios. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.


## Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts and no sign-in, and we run no analytics of our own.


## Your saved game

Your progress (stars, upgrades, spells, records and settings) is saved only on your own device, inside the app's private storage. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.


## Advertising

The free version shows ads between battles, served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (based on your IP address), and basic device and diagnostic information. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your choice before any personalised ads are shown, and you can change it at any time under Settings, Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Google's handling of this data is described at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).


## Purchases

"Remove ads" is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play only tells the game whether you own the purchase.


## Permissions

The game uses internet access only to load ads and check your purchase, and vibration for battle feedback.


## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.


## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
